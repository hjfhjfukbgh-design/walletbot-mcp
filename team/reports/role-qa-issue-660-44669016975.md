## Mission
Prepare a release-grade, evidence-backed smoke/regression matrix for the current Kripto Guru main branch and open PR candidates.

Cover:
- Telegram Mini App boot/auth and safe handling of invalid/expired initData.
- Wallet load, balances, assets, market/search screens and navigation at 360/375/390/430 widths.
- DEMO Manual: open, partial/full close, TP/SL, history, refresh/reconnect and ledger consistency.
- DEMO AI Piper/AI PAPER: funding/session start-stop, virtual positions, decision history and strict separation from Manual DEMO.
- DEMO/LIVE market-mode separation; LIVE must remain disabled/read-only where provider contracts are incomplete.
- Companion general chat, conversation history, fair-use/rate-limit responses and research quota separation.
- Offline/network errors, duplicate taps, retry/replay and stale data states.
- Dark/light theme, safe areas, keyboard/composer and floating navigation overlap.

Inspect current tests and workflows first. Add missing deterministic automated tests only where scope is clear and safe; otherwise produce a prioritized test matrix with exact existing test paths, gaps, reproduction steps and expected results. No real provider orders, Walt writes, secrets, paid services, merge or production deploy. Explicitly mark physical Telegram iOS WebView NOT VERIFIED unless actually tested.
